Why Write Here?

Osagyefo Kwame Nkrumah once said, "The independence of Ghana is meaningless unless it is linked up with the total liberation of Africa." Today, we continue that conversation—reflecting on where we've been and where we're headed.

This platform invites thoughtful commentary, sharp satirical wit, and genuine reflection on Ghana's affairs. Your letter becomes part of our collective dialogue about nation, identity, and progress.

Letters can be personal reflections, policy critiques, humorous take-downs, or hopeful visions. They're published with your permission, becoming part of this ongoing conversation with our nation's past.

💡 Writing Tips

Make your letter memorable:

  • Start with a hook—a question, a quote, or a surprising observation
  • Be specific: reference real events, places, or people
  • Mix emotion with intellect—let readers feel your conviction
  • End with a powerful closing or call to reflection
  • Keep it between 300–1,500 words for readability

✍ What We Love

Letters that resonate most often:

  • Blend humor with serious critique
  • Draw historical parallels thoughtfully
  • Speak from lived experience
  • Propose ideas, not just complaints
  • Celebrate Ghana while challenging her to do better
